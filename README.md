## About
*One Piece is a Japanese manga series written and illustrated by Eiichiro Oda. It follows the adventures of Monkey D. Luffy and his crew, the Straw Hats, as he searches for the legendary treasure known as the "One Piece" to become the next King of the Pirates.*

## The Core Story

**• The Goal:** *Luffy dreams of finding the legendary treasure known as the One Piece, which was left behind on the final island (Laugh Tale) by the late Pirate King, Gol D. Roger. Before his execution, Roger’s dying words launched a massive worldwide "Great Pirate Era".*

**• The Main Character:**  *Luffy accidentally ate a mystical Devil Fruit (the Gum-Gum Fruit / Gomu Gomu no Mi) as a child, which gave his body the properties of rubber, though it permanently robbed him of his ability to swim.*
